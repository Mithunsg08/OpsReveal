Core Mechanics

How Businesses Suffer Unseen Capital Leakage

1

Unnoticed Vendor Cost-Price Creep

Suppliers rarely raise unit prices all at once. Instead, cost-price hikes occur incrementally across thousands of Goods Received Notes (GRNs) over 3 to 12 months. Without cross-referencing historical baselines, accounts payable teams unintentionally approve millions in overcharges.

2

Receiving Shortages & Expiry Dumping

Inward warehouse receiving is a prime vulnerability. Discrepancies between purchase order quantities and actual physical quantities, combined with short-dated inventory near expiry, result in immediate write-off burdens passed directly to the retailer.

3

Register Overrides & Floor Price Breaches

At the point of sale, unauthorized register discounts, override codes, and cashier-level price modifications erode gross margins. Individual line items sold below minimum allowed margins compound into massive systemic financial drain.

4

Uncoordinated Write-Off & Shrink Discrepancies

Spoilage, damage, and sampling logs are frequently kept in silos. Without automated audit engines mapping write-offs against procurement volumes, repeat vendor defect patterns and internal inventory leakage remain undetected.
