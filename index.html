Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Why do you strictly enforce format matching?

Because all parsing and mathematical audit algorithms execute locally on your device in browser memory, your data must strictly match the downloaded template structure. This ensures error-free calculations without sending any files to external cloud servers.

What data is sent to Cloudflare (EU)?

Only high-level, non-identifying summary statistics (e.g., total monetary leakage identified and line counts) are stored in Cloudflare (EU) to populate your dashboard history. Raw file contents, transaction records, and file names are never sent to or saved in the cloud.

What are the eligibility requirements for the 115% ROI Guarantee?

To qualify for the 115% ROI Guarantee on the Professional plan, your account must process a minimum of 75,000 rows or audit at least 75,000 individual transactions during the billing month.
