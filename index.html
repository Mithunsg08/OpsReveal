Comprehensive Loss Audit Engine

5 Targeted Loss Prevention Algorithms

Purpose-built detection algorithms designed to capture hidden financial leakages across the retail supply chain.

01

GRN / Receiving Audit

Detects vendor overcharges, price manipulation, and shelf-life compliance failures at receiving.

  • •Cost Price (CP) Spikes: Flags unexpected cost price increases on identical MRP items.
  • •Same-Day Multi-CP Fraud: Identifies different CP rates billed on the same receiving date.
  • •Expiry Compliance: Blocks stock arriving near or past acceptable expiration thresholds.
02

Sales Margin Audit

Scans register transactions to eliminate negative-margin sales and POS override abuses.

  • •Negative Margin Sales: Flags items sold below acquisition cost.
  • •Discount Override Audits: Detects unauthorized cashier discounts and promo misuse.
03

Write-Off & Shrink Audit

Summarizes total inventory losses across damaged, expired, and stolen goods.

  • •Damage & Spoilage Summaries: Quantifies monetary loss by waste classification.
  • •Expiry Waste Tracking: Categorizes shrink from unsold expired products.
04

Stock Ageing Audit

Combines GRN receipt dates with Quantity-on-Hand (QOH) to identify dead inventory.

  • •Receiving Date Tracking: Maps working capital locked in stale inventory.
  • •Stagnant Stock Alerts: Identifies SKUs sitting un-moved beyond target holding periods.
05

GRN vs. Write-Off Cross-Audit

Cross-references procurement intake directly with waste write-offs to uncover chronic over-ordering.

  • •Procurement Optimization: Correlates GRN quantities with waste volume to optimize supplier MOQs.
  • •Capital Recovery: Provides data-backed procurement adjustments to cut recurring waste.

How does OpsReveal maintain zero data risk?

OpsReveal processes all raw spreadsheets directly inside your browser memory using WebAssembly (Wasm) and client-side JavaScript. Raw spreadsheets containing cost prices, sales volumes, and vendor names never cross the internet.

What file formats are supported?

OpsReveal natively supports CSV, XLSX, and XLS exports from major ERP and POS platforms including SAP, Oracle Retail, LS Retail, Tally, Microsoft Dynamics, and custom POS CSV dumps.
