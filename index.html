Targeted Loss Prevention Algorithms

Comprehensive algorithmic coverage built to identify vendor overcharges, unit price discrepancies, and inventory loss.

Goods Received Note (GRN) Shortage Audit

  • •Cost-Price Spike Detection: Catches suppliers inflating item cost prices on identical products mid-contract.
  • •Historical Price Baselines: Cross-references 2 to 12 months of procurement history to detect gradual price creep.
  • •Expiry Audits: Flags shipments accepted into inventory near or past expiration thresholds.
  • •Shortage Audits: Identifies discrepancies between PO quantities and actual receiving lines.

Sales & Margin Compression Audit

Scans store transactions against procurement cost baselines to reveal unapproved register discounts and negative-margin line items.

  • •Floor Price Violations: Flags register overrides sold below preset minimum allowed prices.
  • •Margin Erosion Tracking: Pinpoints store locations and cashier terminals operating under baseline margins.

Write-Off & Shrink Discrepancies

Consolidates shrink, spoilage, damage, and expired inventory logs into actionable replenishment optimization metrics.

  • •Shrink Valuation: Instantly calculates monetary loss categorized by product lines.
  • •Procurement Trimming: Provides order reduction recommendations on high-damage SKUs.
