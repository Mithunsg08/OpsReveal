Frequently Asked Questions

Loss Prevention & Security Insights

Why is downloading and populating the standard template recommended?

Downloading our standard audit templates and adding your store data directly into them guarantees seamless format compatibility with our client-side audit engine. More importantly, uploading the same standardized file prevents your original internal file names from ever being transmitted or exposed.

How does OpsReveal detect vendor fraud and margin leakage?

OpsReveal compares your actual receiving cost prices against contract rules and sales prices directly inside local browser RAM. It flags unauthorized vendor price increases, shelf-life shortfalls, negative sales margins, and unapproved cashier overrides instantly.

What data is transmitted to Cloudflare (EU)?

Only high-level non-identifying aggregated metrics (e.g., total calculated financial leakage and line item counts) are saved in Cloudflare (EU) to populate your web console history. Raw store transaction data, item codes, and file names are never saved or sent to external servers.
