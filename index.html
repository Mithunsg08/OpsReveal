What OpsReveal Detects Out of the Box

Three distinct analysis modules built specifically for retail, inventory, and procurement workflows.

Receiving (GRN) Leakages

  • •CP Spike Audits: Identifies suppliers stealthily inflating Cost Price for identical MRP products on the same day.
  • •Historical CP Baseline: Merges multi-month history (2–12 months) and flags any order placed above lowest historical CP.
  • •Month-over-Month Drifting: Highlights month-wise vendor price creeping.
  • •Expiry Compliance: Flags stock accepted at receiving that is already expired or near-expiry.
  • •Margin Compression: Flags SKUs where current GRN gross margin drops below historical averages.

Sales Margin Audit

Scans sales logs against product cost baselines to locate unauthorized discounts or register overrides.

  • •Sub-Average Margin Flags: Pinpoints sales transactions completed below baseline gross margin targets.
  • •Under-Cost Pricing: Highlights negative margin line items before month-end reconciliations.

Write-Off & Replenishment

Consolidates shrink, damage, and expired inventory logs into actionable procurement guidance.

  • •SKU Shrink Summaries: Calculates total value loss per category and item.
  • •Smart Procurement Trimming: Generates automated recommendations to cut order quantities on high-writeoff SKUs.
European Data Sovereignty & Zero Raw Ingestion

Your Proprietary Pricing Data Never Leaves Your Browser

Legacy auditing tools require uploading raw operational files to third-party cloud servers. OpsReveal runs its entire analysis engine client-side inside your local browser instance using WebAssembly and Web Workers.
