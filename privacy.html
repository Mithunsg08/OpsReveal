Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how PreventLoss.org ("we", "us") collects, uses, and safeguards personal information processed through OpsReveal (opsreveal.preventloss.org).

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Name, work email address, company details, and authentication credentials via secure identity providers.
  • Payment & Billing Data: Payment details, credit card numbers, and billing addresses are processed entirely by our Merchant of Record, Paddle.com. PreventLoss.org does not store or handle credit card details directly.
  • System Logs & Telemetry: Upload counts, usage statistics, and system logs necessary to maintain platform operational efficiency.

2. How We Use Information

We use personal data exclusively to deliver and optimize OpsReveal services, verify identity, facilitate customer support, and issue transaction notices.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell personal data. We share necessary operational data only with verified service providers required for core infrastructure, including Paddle.com (Payments & Billing), Firebase (Authentication & Storage), and Cloudflare (API Hosting & Security).

4. Privacy Contact

If you have questions regarding data privacy, account deletion, or your personal information, please reach out to info@preventloss.org or our legal team at legal@preventloss.org.
